How to use it

Open any file above directly in a browser: nothing to install, no account needed. Each mock exam offers two modes:

  • Timed: a running clock, answers hidden until you submit, matching the real exam's pacing.
  • Untimed practice: answers and explanations reveal as you go, better for early-stage learning.

Progress is saved in your browser's local storage only. Nothing is sent anywhere, and nothing carries over between devices or browsers.

How the questions are sourced

All questions, scenarios, and explanations are original, written from scratch and not drawn from IAPP's practice exam. Every question cites the specific BoK v2.1 performance indicator it targets and the relevant Sybex study-guide chapter, so a wrong answer points you straight back to the source material rather than leaving you guessing.
